# Progress Indicators

Progress indicators provide visual feedback for completion status in linear and circular formats.

## Variants

Two components for representing the same determinate `value` (0–100) in different
shapes:

**`ProgressBar`** — a horizontal bar. Best inline or full-width, for uploads,
multi-step forms, and reading progress.

**`ProgressCircle`** — a circular track, often with the percentage in the center.
Best in compact, dashboard-style spots like cards and stat tiles.
