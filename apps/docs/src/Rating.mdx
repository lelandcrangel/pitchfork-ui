# Rating

Rating components communicate average score at a glance with stars or a compact badge.

## Variants

Two display styles for the same score — both are read-only summaries, not inputs:

**`RatingStars`** — a row of (partially filled) stars. Best where space allows a
visual, scannable score, like product listings and reviews.

**`RatingBadge`** — a compact pill showing the numeric value. Best in dense
layouts such as tables, cards, and list rows.
